# Law Firm Website Template

Professional, responsive and reusable website template for lawyers, advocates, attorneys and law firms.

## Features
- Responsive mobile/tablet/desktop layout
- Home, About, Practice Areas, Team, Blog and Contact
- Individual service pages
- Article template
- WhatsApp CTA
- SEO-ready structure
- robots.txt and sitemap.xml
- GitHub Pages compatible
- Generic placeholders for easy customization

## Replace These Placeholders
- Your Law Firm Name
- Your Name
- +92 XXX XXXXXXX
- info@yourlawfirm.com
- Your Office Address
- Your Logo
- Team photos and biographies
- Practice-area content
- Articles
- WhatsApp number

## Technology
HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript.

## Important
This is a generic demonstration template. Replace placeholder legal content with accurate, jurisdiction-specific information before production use.
